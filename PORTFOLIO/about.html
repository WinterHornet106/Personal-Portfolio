Who I am

Fixing the "could've been great" in the world.

I'm Jay Ho — a Product Designer based in Vancouver, BC. I believe the difference between a frustrating product and a beloved one is almost always a design decision someone didn't care enough to get right.

I work at the intersection of research, systems thinking, and craft. I ask "why" too many times and iterate until it feels inevitable. Life's too short for bad design.

Currently studying Interactive Arts & Technology at SFU, where I've been honing the balance between rigor and creativity — designing things that are both sharp and humane.

How I work

Research first, craft always.

Good design starts with uncomfortable questions. I spend time understanding why a problem exists before reaching for solutions — mapping user flows, stress-testing assumptions, and finding the moments where friction sneaks in unnoticed.

From there, it's iteration. I move fast in low-fidelity to find the shape of the right answer, then slow down to make it feel polished and inevitable. Every decision gets justified — not because process is sacred, but because craft deserves reasons.

What I believe

Design is a responsibility.

Clarity over cleverness.
The best interface is the one that disappears. I resist the temptation to add complexity when simplicity is available.

People, not users.
Behind every screen is a human with context, habits, and frustrations. Design that forgets this fails regardless of how it looks.

Details compound.
Small inconsistencies erode trust. Getting the micro-moments right is how you earn the macro-perception of quality.

Ship and learn.
Perfect is the enemy of shipped. I value getting work into the world, learning from it, and iterating with real signal.
